Device Tree Compiler (dtc)

Upstream project:
https://github.com/dgibson/dtc

The Gatito Dtb Pacher build incorporates DTC source code under
GPL-2.0-or-later.

The native build pins DTC to commit:
7a1e017926004ecff5fce62d62d42ce9f3e00082

The build performs a small mechanical source adaptation required by the
in-process bridge: the upstream private fill_fullpaths helper is exported
under the dtbp_fill_fullpaths name. The build also compiles the DTC entry
point with a renamed main symbol and redirects fatal exit() calls to the
Gatito bridge. These adaptations are part of the build integration and do
not change the upstream licensing.

The corresponding DTC source is fetched from the upstream repository at
the pinned commit during the build.
